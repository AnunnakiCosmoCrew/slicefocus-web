SliceFocus and Session, side by side.

Both run a Pomodoro timer on your iPhone and your Mac and keep the two in step. Session builds a record around the timer: what you worked on, a note on what you learned, and analytics. SliceFocus builds the day around it: blocks on a 24-hour clock that the timer follows. Here is what each one does, and who it suits.

Who each one suits

  • Session suits you if the timer is the centre of your work and you want a record of it: categories, a note on each session, and analytics by day, week and month. On the Mac its Pro plan also blocks websites in Brave and Edge as well as Safari and Chrome, and it can be automated with Shortcuts and AppleScript.
  • SliceFocus suits you if you plan the day first and want focus to follow the plan: blocks on a 24-hour clock, work handing over to break and to the next block by itself, and a shield for distracting apps on iPhone and Mac. It is free.

If you block websites in Brave or Edge, or you automate your Mac with Shortcuts and AppleScript, Session is the one to pick. SliceFocus covers Safari and Chrome only, and has no automation.
