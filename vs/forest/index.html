SliceFocus and Forest, side by side.

Both help you stay with one thing for a stretch of time and keep your phone from pulling you away. Forest turns the stretch into a tree you grow; SliceFocus turns it into a block on a day you planned. Here is what each one does, and who it suits.

Who each one suits

  • Forest suits you if you want a single timer with a reward in it: a tree that grows while you focus and dies if you give up. It also runs on Android and in the browser, and it has group sessions with friends.
  • SliceFocus suits you if you plan the day first and want focus to follow the plan: blocks on a 24-hour clock, work handing over to break and to the next block by itself, and a shield for distracting apps and sites on iPhone and Mac.

If you are on Android, or you want to focus together with friends, Forest is the one to pick. SliceFocus is built for the iPhone and the Mac.
